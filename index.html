Privacy Policy — Game Points

Last updated: August 2026

Game Points ("the app") is a private, invite-only application used by a closed group of members to track points during in-person game sessions. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and who can see it.

Who can use this app

Game Points is not available to the general public. New members can only be added by an existing admin of the group. There is no public sign-up.

Information we collect

  • Phone number — used as your login identifier. Entered by an admin when you're added to the group, and used to verify your identity when you log in.
  • Name — provided by the admin who adds you, used to identify you to other members within sessions and reports.
  • Nickname (optional) — you may set your own nickname, shown to other members in place of your name where set.
  • PIN (optional) — you may set a 4-digit PIN to protect your account on this app. It is stored in hashed (not readable) form.
  • Session and points data — records of game sessions you participate in, tokens requested, points entered by admins, and resulting settlement calculations.

How this information is used

All data collected is used solely to operate the app's core functionality: identifying members, tracking session participation, and calculating and displaying settlement results. We do not use your information for advertising, marketing, or any purpose outside the app's stated function.

Who can see your information

  • Other members of the group can see your name or nickname, and your token/points activity within sessions you're both part of.
  • Admins of the group can see all members' names, phone numbers, and session history, in order to manage the group and resolve settlements.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Third-party services

The app uses Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication) to store data and manage sign-in. Firebase processes data on our behalf under Google's own security and privacy practices, and does not use it for its own purposes. No advertising or analytics tracking services are used in this app.

Data retention and removal

Your data is retained for as long as you're an active member of the group, and for a reasonable period afterward to preserve historical session and settlement records for other members. If you'd like your account removed or your data deleted, contact a group admin directly.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be directed to the admin of your group, or to: gamepoints.nrag@gmail.com
